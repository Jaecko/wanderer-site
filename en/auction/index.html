Wanderer Auction

The auction house in one quiet window, never below a merchant

Wanderer Auction is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. It brings the game's own auction house into a single Wanderer window, with every native feature and comfort on top, and one rule above all: you never sell for less than a merchant would give you.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer 0.40 or later, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How do I use the auction house in a Wanderer window?

Open the auction house as usual: Wanderer's window takes the place of the game's, with a link for each page: to sell, to buy, your auctions, your bids. One link brings the game's window back at any time, another returns to Wanderer's, and closing the window closes the auction house.

II

How does it choose a selling price?

Choose an item and its lowest price is looked up at once; a price just below is offered, or yours matched if you are already the cheapest. What you set aside in Wanderer Bags comes first and has its price looked up by itself, and what you keep there is never offered.

A starting bid for items, the duration, the whole stack or a part of it: one click posts it. A right click on an item of your bags chooses it to sell.

III

Will it ever sell below a merchant's price?

Never. The auction house takes a cut on every sale, so Wanderer Auction compares what you would really receive with what a merchant would give. A price that would bring less is never offered and cannot be posted: the window tells you the least worth asking, or to sell the item to a merchant.

The deposit, lost if nothing sells, is shown in orange when it is more than what you would gain over the merchant.

IV

How do I search and buy?

Search by name, or with the game's categories, filters and levels, sorted by price, name or level; your favorites are a right click away. Commodities are bought by the quantity, their total cost said first; items by buyout or by bid. Every purchase and every bid is confirmed before it is made, and what a merchant gives for an item is shown, in green when it is more than its price here.

V

How do I follow my auctions and bids?

Your auctions show in red the ones that are undercut, and say when following is not worth it. What sold waits in your mail. A cancel shows its cost and is asked first. Your bids put the outbid ones first, a new bid or the buyout in one click.

VI

Can I see auction prices away from the auction house?

Prices seen are remembered for the whole account, with how long ago. Wanderer Bags shows them on its sections, and the tooltips show them over an item, unless Auctionator is there to say them itself.

VII

Can I keep a shopping list?

Yes, and it opens anywhere: from the Shopping link of Wanderer Bags, a tab on the merchant's window, Wanderer's menu, a key of yours or /wauction. Add an item with an Alt+click in your bags or a Shift+click while the list is open; quests, recipes (a profession's window sends what you lack) and reminders add theirs. What you carry against what you want is said, and an item leaves the list by itself once you have enough.

At the auction house, the whole list is searched at once, cheapest first. Identical items to sell stand on one line and are posted together, like a stack.

VIII

Can it keep my reagents stocked?

Yes. Mark an item "always on you" with the count you want (maple seeds, ammunition, food): the line never leaves the list. At a merchant who sells it, a +N link beside it buys exactly what is missing.

An option, off by default and also in the list's window, buys it all on arrival: for gold only, never above a cap per visit (5 gold unless you change it), what was bought said in the chat. Hold Shift while opening the merchant to buy nothing.

Questions about Wanderer Auction

Does Wanderer Auction replace Auctionator?

No, it is not meant to. It brings the game's own auction house into a Wanderer window, with comfort on top: the lowest price found, a merchant's price never undercut, your marks from Wanderer Bags. It works beside Auctionator, and the game's window stays one click away.

How does Wanderer Auction compare a price with a merchant's?

Wanderer Auction reads what a merchant gives for the item, takes away the auction house's cut from the price you ask, and never offers or posts a price whose net would be lower. It also warns when the deposit, lost if nothing sells, is more than your gain.

How do I install Wanderer Auction?

Search for Wanderer Auction in the CurseForge app: it installs Wanderer with it. By hand, download Wanderer and Wanderer Auction from CurseForge and unzip both into the Interface, AddOns folder of World of Warcraft Forever (_classic_beta_ on most installs).

Does Wanderer Auction buy or sell anything by itself?

No. Wanderer Auction looks prices up for you, but every sale, purchase, bid and cancel needs your click, and purchases, bids and cancels are confirmed first, as the game requires.

Do I need Wanderer Bags for Wanderer Auction?

No. Wanderer Auction only needs Wanderer 0.40 or later. With Wanderer Bags, your marks (to keep, for the auction house) guide what is offered.

Can I keep the game's auction house window?

Yes. An option in Wanderer's options keeps the game's window, and a link beside it opens Wanderer's whenever you want.

Is Wanderer Auction free?

Yes, completely, like Wanderer. It is translated into 10 languages, and nothing is ever bought, bid or posted without your click.
