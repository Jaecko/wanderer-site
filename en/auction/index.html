Wanderer Auction

The auction house in one quiet window, never below a merchant

Wanderer Auction is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. It brings the game's own auction house into a single Wanderer window, to sell, buy, follow your auctions and read the prices of the last days, and adds a shopping list you can open anywhere. One rule above all: you never sell for less than a merchant would give you.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How does it choose a selling price?

Choose an item and its price is looked up at once. The optimized price sits just under the first credible offer, weighed against the last fourteen days; bait offers far too low to be real are left out, and a line says why that price. Sell fast or the market's price are one choice away.

Type a price as you like, 1.5 or 1g50s or 75s. A price under half of an item's usual value, or over three times, shows in red and is asked before posting, and "Keep as your price" makes yours the least ever offered for that item. What you set aside in Wanderer Bags comes first, identical items are posted together, and Sell everything lines up all that brings more here than at a merchant: Space posts the next one.

II

Will it ever sell below a merchant's price?

Never. The auction house takes a cut on every sale, so Wanderer Auction compares what you would really receive with what a merchant gives. A price that would bring less is raised to the merchant's, and said. A deposit larger than your gain is pointed out.

With Wanderer Bags, an item set aside for the auction house that is surely worth more at a merchant becomes junk. Items the server refuses to post are remembered and no longer offered.

III

How do I search and buy?

Search by name within the game's categories, with rarity, levels, usable items only and your favorites; your last searches wait under the search box. A price shows in green when it is well under its usual price, in red when well over. Commodities are bought by quantity with their total said first, items by buyout or bid, and every purchase and bid is confirmed.

IV

How do I follow my auctions and bids?

The Following tab gathers your auctions and your bids: cheaper elsewhere, the cheapest, sold and on its way by mail, outbid. One action per row: cancel with its cost asked first, bid again at the least possible, or buy out. Take back the undercut gathers the auctions worth reposting in one card, each canceled with its own click, the deposit lost said first.

V

Is now a good time to sell?

The History tab shows an item's prices over the last fourteen days in a small chart, with its market value and today's cheapest, and says it plainly: a good time to sell, the usual price, better to wait, or better at a merchant. Away from the auction house, the last price seen shows in the item's tooltip.

VI

Can I keep a shopping list?

Yes, and it opens anywhere. Add an item from your bags, your quests, your reminders or a profession's window, which sends the reagents you lack and says what the recipe costs against what it sells for. A line can be for once, or always on you with a count: arrows, food, reagents. At the auction house the whole list is searched at once, cheapest first.

At a merchant who sells it, a +N link buys exactly what is missing. If you wish, it is bought on arrival, for gold only and under a cap per visit, and the banker can hand over what your bank holds first. Hold Shift to skip both.

Questions about Wanderer Auction

Does Wanderer Auction replace Auctionator?

It is not meant to. It works beside Auctionator or TradeSkillMaster: when one of them is installed, the game's window stays until you choose Wanderer's, and the tooltip price is left to Auctionator.

Does Wanderer Auction buy or sell anything by itself?

No. Every sale needs your gesture, every purchase, bid and cancel is confirmed first. At a merchant, nothing is bought unless you click +N or turn on buying on arrival, capped per visit.

What if I type a wrong price by mistake?

A price under half of the item's usual value, or over three times, shows in red and is asked again before it is posted, when selling everything too. And no price is ever offered below what a merchant gives.

Why are prices in whole silver?

Because WoW Forever's server refuses copper in auction prices. Wanderer Auction writes every price in whole silver so no sale is refused.

Do I need Wanderer Bags for Wanderer Auction?

No, only Wanderer. With Wanderer Bags, your marks guide what is offered and the shopping list is an Alt+click away.

Can I keep the game's auction house window?

Yes. An option keeps the game's window, and a link beside it opens Wanderer's whenever you want.

Is my shopping list shared between characters?

The list belongs to each character. The prices you have seen are shared by all of them.
