Wanderer Bags

All your bags and your bank in one quiet window

Wanderer Bags is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. It gathers every bag you carry in a single window, sorts what you carry the way you think about it, keeps what matters safe from a careless sale, and brings your bank along, even far from the banker.

Download on CurseForge Download on Wago

A free extension: it needs Wanderer 0.40 or later, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How do I see all my bags in one window in WoW Forever?

Install Wanderer Bags with Wanderer: your bags open in one window drawn like every Wanderer window, following your Wanderer profiles. The squares are the game's own: a right click uses an item, a drag moves it, the tooltips, cooldowns and the glow of new items are exactly as you know them.

Sorted by kind, what you just picked up comes first, then gear, consumables, crafting, quests and the rest, the junk at the end, and your free slots fit in a single square. If you prefer, bag by bag, each slot in its place.

II

How do I find an item quickly?

A search by name or by kind ("quest", "junk"...) lets what does not match step back. The item level shows in the corner of your gear, Wanderer's green arrow marks what is better for the branch you play, and a small coin marks the junk wherever you are.

III

Can my crafting reagents be sorted by profession?

Yes. Crafting is split into a section for each of your professions, then what your other characters use, then what only other professions use, just before the junk: what you may sell, at a glance. Wanderer knows the recipes, and yours as soon as a profession's window has been opened.

IV

How do I stop an item from being sold by mistake?

Alt+click an item and keep it: a padlock appears, and the item is never sold, neither to a merchant nor at the auction house, until you free it. Kept items gather in their own section, at the bottom of your bags.

The same click sets an item aside for the auction house (never sold to a merchant), marks it as junk, or puts it in a section of your own, named as you like. Any section folds with a click on its title.

V

Can I sell my junk safely at a merchant?

At a merchant, a Sell link sits beside the junk and the sections you may sell, with their value. The game's Sell All Junk follows your marks, so what you keep stays in your bags. With Wanderer Auction or Auctionator, a section also shows its value at the auction house.

VI

Can I see my bank from anywhere?

At the banker, your bank opens in its own Wanderer window beside your bags, with your character's tabs and the Warband's, the game's deposit and sorting; a right click moves an item between the two. Away from the banker, one link of your bags shows your bank as you left it.

Questions about Wanderer Bags

Do I need Wanderer for Wanderer Bags?

Yes, Wanderer 0.40 or later. Wanderer Bags is an extension: it uses Wanderer's windows, profiles and item comparison, and its settings sit in Wanderer's options. The CurseForge app installs Wanderer with it.

How do I install Wanderer Bags?

Search for Wanderer Bags in the CurseForge app: it installs Wanderer with it. By hand, download Wanderer and Wanderer Bags from CurseForge or Wago and unzip both into the Interface, AddOns folder of World of Warcraft Forever (_classic_beta_ on most installs).

Does Wanderer Bags show item levels and upgrades?

Yes. Wanderer Bags shows the item level in the corner of your gear and Wanderer's green arrow on what is better for the branch you play, from Wanderer's weights or the Pawn scales you imported.

Does Wanderer Bags handle the Warband bank?

Yes. At the banker, Wanderer Bags shows your character's tabs and the Warband's beside your bags, with the game's own deposit and sorting, and keeps a copy you can look at anywhere.

Can I get the game's bags back?

Yes. One checkbox in Wanderer's options gives the game's bags back, another the game's bank. Nothing else in your game changes.

Does Wanderer Bags work with Auctionator?

Yes. What you keep is hidden from Auctionator's selling list, and what you set aside for the auction house comes first in it.

Is Wanderer Bags free?

Yes, completely, like Wanderer. It is translated into 10 languages, and nothing leaves your game.
