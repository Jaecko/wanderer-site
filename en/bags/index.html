Wanderer Bags

All your bags and your bank in one quiet window

Wanderer Bags is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. It gathers every bag you carry in a single window, sorts what you carry the way you think about it, keeps what matters safe from a careless sale, and brings your bank along, even far from the banker. The squares are the game's own, and one checkbox gives you the game's bags back.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How do I see all my bags in one window in WoW Forever?

Install Wanderer Bags with Wanderer: your bags open in one window drawn like every Wanderer window. The squares are the game's own: a right click uses an item, a drag moves it, the tooltips, cooldowns and the glow of new items are exactly as you know them.

Sorted by kind, what you just picked up comes first, then gear, consumables, crafting, quests and the rest, the junk at the end. Quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags and profession bags each have their own free square, and while the window is open every item keeps its place, so nothing slides under your mouse. If you prefer, bag by bag, each slot in its place. The size of the squares and the number per row are yours.

II

How do I find an item quickly?

A search by name or by kind lets what does not match step back, and says how many your other characters carry. It also knows levels (>40, 30-40), qualities, BoE, equip slots, ! for without and | for or. The item level shows in the corner of your gear, "BoE" on gear that binds when equipped, Wanderer's green arrow marks what is better for the way you play, a small coin marks the junk, and a quest sign the items a quest asks you to carry.

Over any item, a list says which of your characters carry it, in their bags and in their bank. When your bags are nearly full, the free slots name the cheapest things you carry.

III

Can my crafting reagents be sorted by profession?

Yes. Crafting is split into a section for each of your professions, then what your other characters use, then what only other professions use, just before the junk: what you may sell, at a glance. The pieces of your outfits gather in a section of their own.

IV

How do I stop an item from being sold by mistake?

Alt+click an item: a small card offers Keep, Auction, Junk or Bank. A kept item wears a padlock and is never sold, neither to a merchant nor at the auction house. An item for the auction house is never sold to a merchant, a junk item goes with the junk whatever its color, and an item for the bank goes into it at your next visit.

Sections of your own take the names you like, fold with a click, and can be pinned to the top or moved. A Select button acts on many items at once.

V

Can I sell my junk safely at a merchant?

At a merchant, the junk and what only other professions use show their value and a Sell button, which asks first how many items for how much. A merchant buys back only the last 12 items, so bigger sales go by lots of 12, and what is worth over three times more at the auction house is left aside unless you tick the box. The game's own Sell All Junk follows your marks too, so what you keep, your outfits and your quest items always stay in your bags.

VI

Can I see my bank from anywhere?

At the banker, your bank opens beside your bags, sorted the same way, with your character's tabs and the Warband's; a right click moves an item between the two, and what you marked for the bank goes in by itself (hold Shift to skip it). A section's card deposits the whole section, or sends it to the bank at every visit. Away from the banker, one link shows your bank as you left it.

Questions about Wanderer Bags

Can I get the game's bags back?

Yes. One checkbox in Wanderer's options gives the game's bags back, another the game's bank. Nothing else in your game changes.

Do quivers and soul bags count as free slots?

No. Quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags and profession bags each have their own free square with their own count, so the free slots of your bags tell the truth, and the nearly full warning only looks at your ordinary bags.

Does it work with Bagnon, AdiBags or ElvUI's bags?

Yes. When another addon has your bags, Wanderer Bags steps aside by itself, and your marks still guard what is sold at merchants.

Does Wanderer Bags work with Auctionator?

Yes. What you keep is hidden from Auctionator's selling list, what you set aside for the auction house comes first in it, and its prices show on your sections.

Does Wanderer Bags handle the Warband bank?

Yes. At the banker it shows your character's tabs and the Warband's beside your bags, with the game's own deposit and sorting, and keeps a copy you can look at anywhere.

Does it sell anything by itself?

No. Nothing is sold without your click on Sell, and a section's sale is asked first. Only what you marked for the bank moves by itself, and Shift skips it.

Are my marks shared between characters?

No. Marks, sections, their order and folds are each character's own.
