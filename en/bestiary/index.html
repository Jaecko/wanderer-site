Wanderer Bestiary

Every creature of the world, and your story with each

Wanderer Bestiary is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. The creatures you may fight, from the wolves of your first steps to the bosses of the deepest raids, more than 3,600 of them, gather in one quiet window, each with its card: its 3D model, its strength, its spells, where it lives. The bestiary fills itself as you play: a creature you never met stays in shadow until you cross its path. It reads and remembers, and never fights or targets for you.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

Can I discover the creatures as I meet them?

Yes. A creature you never met is a shadow and a question mark. Met once, targeted, under the mouse or by its nameplate, its model and name appear. Killed once, its strength, its blows and its spells are yours to read.

Keep the surprise goes further if you wish: a boss, a rare or an elite you never met hides its level and where it lives, so the world keeps its secrets. A golden line says how much of the world you discovered, and Here how much of your zone you met and killed.

II

What does a creature's card show?

A click on a creature opens its card beside the window, never moving it. Its 3D model turns as you drag it and comes closer with the wheel. Then its levels, rank and kind, its health, blows and armor, its spells, the gold it carries, and whether it can be skinned or pickpocketed.

Where it lives comes with its zones, and a click shows the way there. Under it, your own story with it: how many you killed, the first and the last time, what you looted from it, and whether it ever killed you.

III

Can it keep a hunting log for each character?

Yes. Each character keeps its own: kills, deaths, loot, your series since your last death and your best one. From the first login, the bestiary takes in what the game already counted: your kills and deaths in all, the kind you hunted the most, every dungeon and raid boss you brought down, and some of the creatures that killed you.

Every kill is counted with care: only a creature you or your pet fought and saw fall, never another player's, never the same one twice, and a death at a player's hands is never blamed on a creature. In dungeons and raids, each boss counts when its fight ends.

IV

Which records does it keep?

Records counts your hunt: creatures discovered and killed, your most hunted, your nemesis, the highest level you brought down, your first kill, rares, elites and bosses, your most complete zone, and each kind of creature.

A small notice greets a first elite, rare or boss, and a click opens its card. Quiet milestones follow: a creature killed 25, 100, 500 or 1,000 times, and your kills in all. In the list, round portraits are ringed in the color of their rank, gold for an elite, blue for a rare, red for a boss, with your kills in gold beside them.

V

Can I see my count when I meet a creature?

Yes. A line under a creature's name, in Wanderer's label and the game's tooltip, says how many times you killed it, or that you never met it. Never in a dungeon or a raid.

Here shows the creatures of the zone you stand in, All every one, with a search and a kind. With a controller, the cross moves through the lists, its left and right turn the model and the right stick brings it closer.

VI

Does it work with Wanderer Quests and Wanderer Maps?

Hand in hand. With Wanderer Quests, a creature named on a quest's sheet, the one to kill or the one that drops what it asks, opens its card beside the sheet. With Wanderer Maps, the creatures your quests ask for on a map are listed in the Atlas, and On the atlas draws where a creature lives as soft red zones, the rest of the map stepping aside meanwhile.

Coming from KillTrack? Records offers to bring your counts while KillTrack is loaded, shows what would change first, never lowers a count, and the import can be taken back.

Questions about Wanderer Bestiary

Does it fight or target for me?

Never. It reads and remembers: no target chosen, no attack, nothing sent. Your bestiary is a book, not a helper in a fight.

Does it slow down my game?

No. The creatures are read only when the window needs them, and what you loot stays short: twelve items per creature.

Is my hunting log shared between characters?

No. Each character keeps its own log, its own records and its own settings.

How do I open Wanderer Bestiary?

From Wanderer's bar and menu, its options page, a key of yours in the game's key bindings, or by typing /wbestiary.

Where does its data come from?

The creatures, their strength, spells and places come from the CMaNGOS classic database and pfQuest, credited in the addon. Wanderer Bestiary is free software under the GPL 3.0.
