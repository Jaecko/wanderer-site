Wanderer Campfire Game

A little game by the fire, fed by your journey

Wanderer Campfire Game is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. A campfire grows tier after tier, companions gather around it and set out on expeditions across Azeroth in real time. What you do in the game makes it stronger; what it gives stays in its own world.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How does the camp grow?

The fire makes embers all the time, even while you are away (twelve hours, more with the ashes). Stoke it with a click, buy its producers and their upgrades. With embers and renown it grows through seven tiers, each bringing room for more companions and more expeditions at once; the seventh, the eternal blaze, never ends, each of its levels asking more.

Six buildings, each up to five levels: a kitchen for provisions, a forge for strength, an apothecary for wounds, tents for room, a storehouse for reserves, a watchtower for what comes back.

II

What is a new fire?

Once a fire has grown, let it go out for its ashes. Only the fire begins again: the tier, the buildings, the companions, the expeditions and the resources stay. Each ash makes every fire after it stronger, for good, and between two fires, and only then, they are spent in the ashes' tree.

III

Who are the companions?

Each companion is an NPC of the game, of a race of your faction their class allows, ringed in their class color: a protector, a healer or a fighter, with a trait of their own. Their sheet shows them standing in 3D between seven pieces of real game gear, with their strength, statistics, talents and journey.

They level up on the road, choose a talent at levels 20, 40 and 60, come in three rarities, and a faithful companion is promoted in time. Won expeditions bring gear: uncommon in zones, rare in dungeons, epic in raids. An exalted reputation brings a champion of that faction to your fire.

IV

How do the expeditions work?

Choose a place, how long and who goes: forty-four zones, twenty-three dungeons and seven raids, WoW Forever's own among them. The odds, the provisions, what it should bring and what each companion would learn are said before you leave. Each place holds its threats, and companions who know a spell of their class to counter them raise the odds; one click picks the best party.

Contracts on the board offer a road with its own reward, and one you have seen stays until you take it. A standing order, Set out again on each return, sends the same party down the same road each time it comes back, until someone is hurt or provisions run short. Each party's face moves along the map of the roads.

V

What happens when my companions come back?

One card greets you at the camp: what the fire made and the expeditions back, collected in one click or read one by one. Each report shows the place, victory or defeat, the experience gained, the spoils and the gear found, and tells the story of the road: which companion held off which threat, with what spell, and why a lost road was lost.

A long absence is not wasted. An absence longer than the fire burns leaves a rested camp, and the next roads teach half as much again. Last week's chest waits for you, and the notice of a return waits until you are out of a fight or a dungeon.

VI

How does my real journey count?

A zone opens to expeditions when your character has its exploration achievement. Your level opens the fire's tiers and the dungeons. Your professions strengthen the camp, and the quartermaster asks for goods of your professions: have them in your bags or your bank, they stay yours.

Each quest you hand in brings renown, each fish caught and each dish cooked brings provisions. A boss you kill in a dungeon or a raid makes it lived: its expeditions bring a quarter more, for good.

Sit by a real campfire of the game and everything the camp makes grows by half while you stay there.

VII

What happens by the fire?

Every few hours something happens by the fire: a peddler, a storm, wolves, a bard. You choose, and live with it. Some stories go on over three nights.

Trophies to find in every place, feats of the camp, and champions of your exalted factions.

Questions about Wanderer Campfire Game

Does Wanderer Campfire Game give anything in the real game?

No. It only reads what your character has done (achievements, level, professions, items in your bags); everything it gives stays in the game by the fire.

Does it slow down my game?

No. Nothing runs while its window is closed: the time gone by is counted when you look, and one timer only waits for the next expedition to come back.

Do I need to log in often?

No. Expeditions last from fifteen minutes to a whole day, a standing order sends a party out again by itself, contracts you have seen stay on the board, and a long absence leaves a rested camp. Come back when you like.

Is the camp shared between my characters?

No. Each character has its own camp, fed by its own journey.

How do I open it?

From Wanderer's menu, a key of yours in the game's key bindings, or /wcampfire.
