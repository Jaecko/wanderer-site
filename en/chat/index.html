Wanderer Chat

A chat that stays quiet and brings up what is for you

Wanderer Chat is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. The game's own chat is dressed as glass: no frame at rest, lines on a light veil that fade, everything back under the mouse. Your name shows in gold, your guild and group stand out, and a small card comes only when something is really for you. Nothing is ever sent without your own Enter.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

Can the chat fade away when nothing happens?

Yes. At rest the chat has no frame, no tabs, no buttons: each line sits on a soft veil and fades after twenty seconds, or one or two minutes, or never. Rest the mouse on it or start typing and a quiet ground, the tabs and a thin scroll bar come back.

Each line reads at a glance: the time in its own column, the chat as one colored letter, the name in its color. Long channel names get shorter, one text size serves every chat window, and scrolled up, one click brings you back to the latest.

II

How do I know when someone says my name?

A line naming you shows in gold, and stays a minute longer. If it was said where you could not read it, in another tab, while the chat had faded or while you were busy, a card beside the chat shows it with a button to answer, and a quiet sound can tell you, once a minute at most.

Give it your other names too, a nickname or your main's: they show in gold and alert you like your own.

When someone greets the guild or says goodbye, a card offers an answer by name that fits the hour, written for you and sent only by your Enter. A whisper you have not answered waits in a small line above the chat.

III

Can it calm the trade and general channels?

Yes. The same words said again within a minute and obvious gold selling are kept out of the public channels, never your lines, your friends, your guild, your group or your whispers. A quiet line says how many were hidden, and a click shows them.

Words of your own can keep lines out of the public channels as well, and a stranger whispering gold for sale makes no sound and opens no window. Everything kept out waits in one list, ready to read or copy, never lost.

If you wish, a card comes when someone seeks players for a dungeon of your level, short names like RFC or BRD understood, or when one of your own watched words is said.

IV

Can my chat tabs show what I missed?

Group, Guild, Whispers and Channels can sit beside General in one click, each with only its own messages. A small gold count says what was said while a tab was hidden, a gold dot when a line there names you. Each tab keeps its last lines after a reload, and your sent messages come back with the arrow keys.

A search finds words in a tab's lines, each found in gold, Enter for the older one. From the menu of a name, Copy the line gives that line alone, ready to copy.

V

Can I see who someone is from the chat?

Rest the mouse on a name: a card shows their level, class, zone, guild and rank, whether they are a friend or one of your characters, and your private note on them, only from what the game already knows. The name's menu gains Greet, Reply and Quote this line.

VI

Can whispers read like a conversation?

If you turn it on, your whispers move to a window of their own, one conversation per person in bubbles, yours in gold. A new message opens it without taking your keyboard, never during a fight. Pin a conversation, search it, invite, add a friend, all from its menu.

The words you started stay in their conversation when you switch to another one or close the window.

Questions about Wanderer Chat

Does it replace the game's chat?

No. The game's chat engine stays, Wanderer Chat only dresses it and rewrites lines after the game builds them. One checkbox gives the chat back entirely to the game.

Does it work with ElvUI, Prat or Chattynator?

Yes. When one of them dresses the chat, Wanderer Chat's look steps aside and keeps only what does not clash. With WIM, your whispers stay WIM's.

Does it send anything by itself?

Never. Greetings and answers are written in your chat line and sent only by your Enter, and it never does a /who on its own.

Are my settings and notes shared between characters?

No. Settings, notes, kept lines and conversations belong to each character.

How do I open my private messages?

From Wanderer's menu, a key of yours, or by typing /wchat.
