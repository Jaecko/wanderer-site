Wanderer Mail

Your mailbox summed up, your letters taken in one click

Wanderer Mail is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. A card beside the game's mailbox says what it holds and takes the letters you choose; a small list fills the recipient when you write. It replaces nothing: the mail window stays the game's, and Wanderer Mail never deletes, sends or pays anything by itself.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

Can I see what my mailbox holds at a glance?

Yes. A card beside the mailbox gives the number of letters, the gold inside, the letters to pay on delivery, your free bag slots and the first letter about to go back. The auction house's letters are sorted apart, sold, back to you, won, outbid, and when the server keeps more than fifty, the card says how many more wait. On each letter of the inbox, a small word says what happens when its days are over: back to its sender, or destroyed.

II

How do I take all my auction mail at once?

Each block has its own Take these button: the auction house's letters only, or the players' letters only. Take the gold takes just the gold. The button counts as it goes and stops with a click; afterwards the card says what was taken. Shift+click on a letter takes it alone; Ctrl+click on a player's letter sends it back, after a question.

Full bags no longer stop everything: an item joins a stack of its own kind when it can, one free slot is kept, and the rest waits in its letter while the gold still comes. Beyond fifty letters, once the server brings the next ones, one click goes on. A letter you would have to pay for is never taken.

III

Can I send mail to my alts without typing their names?

Yes. A small arrow at the end of the recipient field opens a list: your other characters of this realm and side, with their class color and level, then the last players you wrote to. One click writes the name; the field stays yours. Your friends and your guild members online are in the list too.

When gold or items go to a name you do not know, or when one of your own characters is asked to pay on delivery, a quiet word shows in the field. The game's Send button stays the only one that sends.

IV

Will I know when a letter is about to expire?

Yes. At login, a quiet notice says when one of your other characters has a letter with gold or items leaving within three days, and Wanderer's characters window shows it on their line. A letter of yours going back within a day joins your reminders.

Questions about Wanderer Mail

Does it work with Postal?

Yes. When Postal or TradeSkillMaster's mailing is there, Wanderer Mail's take buttons step aside; the card and the recipient list stay.

Can it delete or send letters by itself?

Never. It never deletes, sends or pays anything: every take starts from your click.

How do I check my mail from anywhere?

Type /wmail: it says whether a letter waits for you.
