Wanderer Maps

Your notes, your route and your way, on the game's own map

Wanderer Maps is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. It adds your own notes and a route of steps to the world map, a small arrow toward the step you follow, and a way guided through your flight paths, the boats, zeppelins and tram. The continents become one seamless map, and each zone says its levels and the fishing it asks. Everything is drawn by the game's own map, so its zoom, pins and clicks stay as you know them.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How do I put my own notes on the world map?

Alt+click anywhere on the map: a small card opens where you clicked, with a name and one of eight signs (a place, a star, a treasure, herbs, ore, danger, a meeting, a question). Enter saves it, Shift+Enter saves and follows it, Ctrl+Enter puts it on your route.

A window gathers your route and your notes in two tabs, with their distances kept fresh, and lists the nearest inn, flight master, mailbox and trainer you know, one click to follow. A route you like, a round of herbs or of mailboxes, can be kept under a name and followed again later.

A Notes button on the world map shows or hides your notes by sign, or all at once, and the minimap follows. On a continent's map they become small dots, grouped by zone when many, and the Notes tab removes a whole sign or zone at once, asked first.

II

Can an arrow lead me to my next step?

Yes. A small arrow you place where you like points toward the step you follow, with the distance in yards: gold on course, amber when the point is behind you. Near a step it ticks itself and the next one is followed. The game's own floating marker and a dotted line on the minimap follow the same point. It also gives the time of arrival and, on a route, the time for all of it.

When no step is followed, the arrow points to the pin the game keeps on your map: yours, a place from Wanderer Quests, a trainer. A right click on it gives the next step, a new plan of the way, or the window.

After a death, the arrow points to your corpse while you are a ghost, and your route comes back at resurrection.

III

Does it count flight paths, boats and my hearthstone?

Yes. To reach a step, the guided way takes the quickest road through your feet, the flight paths this character has found, the boats, zeppelins and tram of the original world, and leads you leg by leg: to the flight master, fly to, the boat to.

If you wish, your hearthstone counts too when its inn is known: still cooling down, its wait is counted, and it is only taken when it still comes first.

IV

Can the continent map show every zone in one piece?

Yes. On a continent's map, each zone's own land is drawn where it lies, as sharp as on its own map: one seamless world. The land you have not explored yet shows softened and without colors, and the map's bar says how many areas of the zone you have explored.

Beside the zone's name, its levels in the colors of their difficulty for you and, when you fish, the skill its waters ask: two things WoW Forever does not show. If you like, the inns, mailboxes, banks, auction houses and trainers you have visited get a small mark.

V

How long is my flight?

In flight, the destination's name stands over a slim line from take off to landing: a dot for each stop, yours moving along, and the time left. Times are learned from your own flights, guessed from the distance until then, and also shown in the flight master's map.

A flight master you have not found yet in your zone can be pointed out once, quietly, with one click to go there.

VI

Does it work with Wanderer Quests?

Hand in hand. From Wanderer Quests, a giver or an objective goes on your route in one click, and a Quest route button visits every giver of what you may take and every ender of what you carry, each the nearest from the last. Each quest step says what to do there and ticks itself when it is done.

Questions about Wanderer Maps

Does Wanderer Maps replace TomTom?

It does a similar job in Wanderer's style. If TomTom is installed, its arrow is kept and Maps' arrow steps aside, so the two never fight. The same goes for the arrows of the RestedXP and Zygor guides.

Does it walk or fly for me?

No. It shows the way, the road is yours: nothing walks, mounts or flies for you, and nothing is drawn over the world beyond the game's own marker.

Are my notes shared between characters?

Your notes and your route belong to each character. What the world teaches (flight times, the services of a town) is kept for all of them.

Can I turn it all off at once?

Yes. One checkbox in Wanderer's options turns off everything that runs outside the window: the arrow, the pins, the flight line.

How do I open Wanderer Maps?

From Wanderer's menu, a key of yours in the game's key bindings, or by typing /wmaps.
