Wanderer Outfits

The right piece on at the right moment, and off again after

Wanderer Outfits is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. A Carrot on a Stick, a fishing hat, an Argent Dawn trinket help only at one moment, so they stay in your bags: you forget them on the mount, then forget to take them off before the dungeon. Outfits remembers for you. An outfit can be just a few pieces tied to a moment: it goes on when the moment comes and gives back what you wore when it ends. Whole outfits are worn in one click too.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How do I wear my Carrot on a Stick automatically when I mount?

Tie an outfit to a moment: on a mount, fishing, in chosen zones, in a dungeon or a raid, in town or at an inn. It goes on when the moment comes, and what it changed comes back when it ends: the Carrot on a Stick as you mount, your usual trinket as you dismount.

Only the slots the outfit takes are touched. A piece you changed yourself meanwhile stays yours, never taken back. Nothing changes during a fight, and Shift held as a moment comes lets it pass. The first time an outfit goes on by itself, a small notice says why.

II

How do I make an outfit of only a few pieces?

The quickest way: wear the pieces, press For a moment and choose the moment. The outfit takes the pieces that moment usually wants (trinkets, gloves and boots on a mount; hat, gloves and boots for fishing), and its card opens: a click on a piece leaves it aside or takes it again.

Carry a piece made for a moment, such as a Carrot on a Stick, a Lucky Fishing Hat, Nat Pagle's boots or an Argent Dawn trinket, and no outfit takes it yet: once for each piece, a notice offers the outfit. One click makes it, and what you wore is given back at once.

III

Can it remind me to take off my riding trinket before a dungeon?

Yes. Entering a dungeon, or when a ready check comes, a piece made for a moment still on you is said in a small notice, and one click undoes the change. An outfit tied to In a dungeon or raid goes on as you enter.

Undo the last change puts back exactly what you wore before the last change of gear, whatever made it: an outfit, a moment, the pole. Again, and it is redone. Two rings or two trinkets of the same name are told apart by their enchant, gems and suffix, so the right copy goes back on.

IV

Can I save my whole gear and wear it in one click?

Yes. Keep what I wear saves everything under a name: your dungeon gear, your travel clothes. Click it, press 1 to 9 over the window, or use a key of yours that opens a chooser at the mouse, anywhere. An Outfits button waits on the character sheet.

Each outfit says at a glance whether it is worn, which pieces are still to put on, which wait in the bank and which are missing. A missing piece never stops the rest. Put a better piece on by hand and a notice offers to put it in the outfits that took the old one.

V

Can my fishing pole swap in and out by itself?

On a key, the window's pole button or /woutfits pole, the fishing pole comes in hand and your weapons are remembered; press again and they are back. An outfit tied to Fishing adds your hat and boots while the pole is in hand.

If you like, Wanderer's double right click on the water brings the pole at a spot you have fished from, and your weapons come back once you are done: no line for a while, walked away, mounted, swimming, a fight or another zone. Both fishing options are off until you turn them on.

VI

Can I sell a piece of my outfits by mistake?

No. Your outfits' pieces are named in their tooltip, never sold as junk and never put up for auction by Wanderer Auction. With Wanderer Bags they get a section of their own, and at the banker an outfit's card takes its pieces from the bank in one click.

Questions about Wanderer Outfits

Will it change my gear when I do not want it to?

No. Nothing goes on by itself until you tie an outfit to a moment, the fishing options are off, nothing changes during a fight, and one checkbox turns it all off. With ItemRack or Naowh Forever's automatic gear sets, Outfits steps aside so two addons never dress you at the same moment.

Does it use the game's equipment manager?

Yes, when your client has one: your outfits are found in the game's window too. Otherwise Wanderer keeps them for each character.

Are outfits shared between characters?

No, each character has its own outfits and its own settings.

How do I open Wanderer Outfits?

From Wanderer's menu, the button on the character sheet, a key of yours in the game's key bindings, or /woutfits. /woutfits pole takes the pole in hand, /woutfits undo undoes the last change.
