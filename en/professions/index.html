Wanderer Professions

Your recipes on one calm workbench

Wanderer Professions is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. In place of the game's long grey table, your recipes stand on one workbench: every recipe with its icon, the one you choose shown large, its reagents laid out as cards, and only the two or three crafts worth doing next. It says what it knows and where that comes from. The game's own window stays one click away, and nothing is ever made without a click of yours.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How do I see all my recipes with their icons in WoW Forever?

The key you already use for professions opens the workbench. Your professions that make something stand on a rail, one click from one to another. Recipes come as a list or as a wall of icons: bright when you can make it, grey when you cannot, how many in the corner. You can search by recipe or by reagent. With a controller, the Professions entry of the radial wheel opens the workbench too.

The recipe you choose is shown large: the piece worn on your character when it can be, how it binds and what armor it is, and its skill colors on one bar with your skill marked on it.

II

Which reagents am I missing, and where are they?

Reagents are laid out as cards: what your bags hold, what is missing in plain words, what waits in the bank or, with Wanderer Bags, on another character. A reagent you can make yourself takes you to its recipe, or shows as a tree.

What your bags lack can be sent to Wanderer Auction's shopping list in one click.

III

Can I queue crafts and stop them?

While you craft, a progress bar shows what is left and how long, with a way to stop. What was interrupted is one click from going on.

A queue keeps crafts for later, and you start it yourself: nothing is ever made without a click of yours.

IV

What should I craft next to raise my skill?

Two or three crafts for your next points, never a route: the surest points first, then what you can already make. A slider shows the colors and the suggestions at another skill, and nothing changes in the game.

The next rank says the skill and level it asks, and you are told when a trainer can teach it.

V

Where do the recipes come from, and are they right for Forever?

The game comes first. Each recipe says if it was seen in the game, comes from a database of the classic world, or has an unknown source. Forever may have changed a recipe: what comes from the database says so, and nothing is guessed.

Recipes still to learn say where they come from, with a way to go there with Wanderer Maps or a pin on the map.

VI

How do I plan what to buy with Merchant's Favor?

Every recipe it buys, its cost and the reputation it asks, what you can pay now, and a goal to save for. What a merchant really asks replaces what the data says.

VII

Does it know camp objects, Writs and specializations?

Camp objects are sorted by tier: the ones you know, what you can make now and the Blueprint each one asks. A Craftsman's Writ in your bags says what it asks, whether you can make it, and where to hand it in when that is known.

Before you specialize, each path says what it opens, with a clear warning: it is a choice that closes the others.

VIII

Which of my characters can make this?

One page gathers your characters' professions and cooldowns, and "who can make this?" searches across all of them.

Guild crafters, if you want: this is off by default. Once on, you can ask who can make a recipe with one click, and only members who switched it on answer.

Questions about Wanderer Professions

Does it replace the game's profession window?

It opens in its place, and the game's own window stays one click away. Wanderer's options let you keep the game's window and your professions key as they were.

Does it craft for me?

No. Nothing is ever made without a click of yours, and a queue only starts when you start it. It suggests two or three crafts and never plans a route.

Which addons does it work with?

It needs Wanderer. Wanderer Bags, Wanderer Auction and Wanderer Maps are optional: reagents on your other characters, the shopping list, and the way to a recipe still to learn.

Where does its data come from?

From the game's tables, the CMaNGOS database (GPL v3) and pfQuest by Shagu (MIT, for zones); Favor costs and other Forever facts come from public guides. Wanderer Professions is free software under the GPL 3.0 or later.

How do I open Wanderer Professions?

With your professions key, from Wanderer's menu, or by typing /wprofessions. Its settings are in Wanderer's options.

Every quest of your class, your dungeons and your zones, in chains, a sheet for each, and a calm list of the quests you follow.

The Atlas, a big map with your quests as zones, your notes and route, an arrow toward your next step, flights and boats counted.

What your trainer holds

The right piece, at the right moment

Your riding, fishing and dungeon pieces on at the right moment and off after, by themselves. Whole outfits in one click too.

A glass chat that fades, your name in gold, a card when someone names you, quieter channels and whispers as conversations.

A small idle game by the fire: companions on real-time expeditions, and your real journey makes the camp stronger.
