Wanderer Quests

The quests the world hides from you, in one quiet window

Wanderer Quests is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. It shows every quest of your class, of every dungeon and raid and of every zone, each with the quests that lead to it, and where you stand with each. It is not an arrow over the world: it is a book you open to plan your way.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer 0.40 or later, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

Which quests does Wanderer Quests show?

Three tabs: your class, its own quests by bands of levels; dungeons and raids, the quests of each and every quest that leads to them; zones, every zone with quests for you, the one you stand in chosen first.

Only what is for you: your race, your class, your side. Each place says its levels and how many of its quests you have done.

II

How do I see a quest chain?

A place's quests are drawn as a tree: the quests that open others above them, wherever they start, in the colors of their difficulty. The quest you need to open a dungeon chain, the class quest no one tells you about, the one you skipped three zones ago: they are all there.

Each says if it is done, in your log, to take, after another, another choice taken, or too high for now. A quest the game let you skip is said passed.

III

What does a quest's sheet show?

A click on a quest opens its sheet beside the window: what to do, in its own words, and its story; the items it asks with how many you already carry, the creatures or objects to find; who gives it and who takes it back, with a pin on your map for each.

Read ahead: a quest still far off is prepared as well as one in your log.

IV

Can I open a quest from the game's quest log?

Yes. Beside a quest's details in the game's quest log, an Open in Quests button shows it here, in its tab and its place, its sheet open. The same line waits in the right-click menus of the log and of the objective tracker.

V

Does it show a quest's rewards?

Yes, as the game gives them, every choice included, learned when you see the quest. A green arrow marks the quests whose reward is better for the branch you play, and the sheet says what comes next and who gives it. Near a dungeon, an option (off) quietly says how many of its quests you could still take.

VI

Does it work with the other Wanderer addons?

What a quest asks goes to Wanderer's reminders in one click, with how many you carry as you gather it. Its items go to the shopping list of Wanderer Auction, searched all at once at the auction house.

Questions about Wanderer Quests

Do I need Questie or another quest addon?

No. Everything Wanderer Quests knows comes with it, from the CMaNGOS classic database and pfQuest. Only Wanderer is needed.

Does Wanderer Quests slow down my game?

No. Its data is read only when you first open the window, only your language is loaded, and where you stand is always asked of the game, never saved apart.

Does Wanderer Quests draw arrows or markers over the world?

No. It shows nothing over the world: a pin goes on your map only when you ask for one.

How do I open Wanderer Quests?

From Wanderer's minimap menu, its options page, or by typing /wquests.

Does it know the quests added by WoW Forever?

Not yet: it knows the quests of the original game. Tell us which new ones you meet in the comments on CurseForge.

Is Wanderer Quests free?

Yes, completely, like Wanderer. It is free software under the GPL-3.0, translated into 10 languages.
