Wanderer Quests

The quests the world hides from you, in one quiet window

Wanderer Quests is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. Every quest of your class, of every dungeon and raid and of every zone, each with the quests that lead to it, and where you stand with each. Click one and its sheet tells you what to do, who gives it, who takes it back and what it brings you. On your screen, a calm list of the quests you follow, in your own order, with one quest in front. No other quest database is needed: everything ships with it.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

Which quests does Wanderer Quests show?

Five tabs: your class, by bands of levels; dungeons and raids, with every quest that leads to them; zones, the one you stand in chosen first; the libraries' books; and your gear. Only what is for you: your race, your class, your side.

Each place sums itself up with its levels and its counts: to take, in your log, done. A search box finds any quest by name in every tab at once, and the done ones can step aside.

II

How do I see a quest chain?

A place's quests are drawn as a tree: the quests that open others above them, wherever they start, in the colors of their difficulty for you. The quest that opens a dungeon chain, the class quest no one tells you about, the one you skipped three zones ago: they are all there.

Each says where you stand: done, in your log, to take, after another, another choice taken, too high for now, or passed when the game let you skip it.

III

What does a quest's sheet show?

A click on a quest opens its sheet beside the window: its step in the chain, a Ready line (your level, the quests before, what you carry), what it asks with how many you already have and the creatures that drop it, who gives it and who takes it back.

One main button follows where you stand: go to the giver, go to the objective, go hand it in. The rewards are the ones Forever really gives, learned as you see them, with a green arrow when one is better for you. If you like, the story of a quest you have not taken stays folded, so you hear it from its giver first.

IV

Can I reorder my tracked quests and focus on one?

Yes. Wanderer Quests can take the place of the game's list of followed quests. One quest stands in front, at the top: bigger, with a star and its objectives always shown. A click puts a quest in front, a click again lets it go, and nothing else ever changes it. The game follows that quest on its map, even when one of its objectives changes.

The others follow in your own order: drag one by its handle or move it from its right click, and Arrange now orders the list once, by level, by distance or ready to hand in first. The zone you stand in comes first, the dungeons apart, each zone folded as you left it. A quest ready says to whom it goes, its item waits on its line, and the list stays faded at rest and quieter in a fight.

V

Can it show the quests I can take on the world map?

Yes. The game's own "!" appears at each giver with a quest you could take now, from three levels below yours to three above, and the older ones too if you wish, greyed. Every giver, objective and ender is a click away: followed by the arrow of Wanderer Maps, or the game's own pin.

Every dungeon and raid has a button to pin its entrance on your map, at the door itself, even where the game gives none. Near a dungeon, a quiet notice can say how many of its quests you could still take first.

VI

Which quests give better gear for me?

The Gear tab sets two character sheets side by side: what you wear, and for each slot the best piece a quest can give you, judged for the way you play, with its gain and your characteristics if you wore them all. A click on a piece opens its quest.

VII

Can it help with the library books of WoW Forever?

Yes. The Books tab lists every library book: where it lies, whether it waits in your bags or your bank, whether it is handed in, and the next reward. With Wanderer Maps, one button makes a route through every book still to find, and reading a book in Wanderer's reader says who takes it.

VIII

Does it work with the other Wanderer addons?

With Wanderer Maps, one button makes a quest route through the givers of what you may take and the enders of what you carry, each the nearest from the last, and each step ticks itself. What a quest asks goes to Wanderer's reminders in one click, its items to the shopping list of Wanderer Auction.

Wanderer's label marks the creatures that drop an item one of your quests asks for, and an Open in Quests line waits in the game's quest log and objective tracker.

Questions about Wanderer Quests

Do I need Questie or another quest addon?

No. Everything Wanderer Quests knows comes with it. If you use Questie or Kaliel's Tracker anyway, Quests steps aside: its marks leave the map to Questie's givers, and its list of followed quests leaves the screen to their tracker.

Does it know the quests added by WoW Forever?

Yes, as you meet them: a quest Forever added is kept as soon as you see its giver, then shows in the lists and the search like the others. Rewards are learned the same way, so what the game shows always wins.

Does Wanderer Quests slow down my game?

No. Its data is read only when you first open the window, only English and your language are loaded, and nothing is drawn on the map during a fight.

Does it accept or turn in quests for me?

No. It shows and guides, it never plays: no automatic accept, no turn in, and a map link only ever goes to your chat box, never sent.

How do I open Wanderer Quests?

From Wanderer's menu, its options page, a key of yours in the game's key bindings, or by typing /wquests.

Where does its data come from?

From the CMaNGOS classic database and pfQuest, dungeon doors from Endless Map, library books from Wowdee, all credited. Wanderer Quests is free software under the GPL 3.0.
