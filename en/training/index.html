Wanderer Training

Know what your trainer holds for you, and what it will cost

Wanderer Training is a free extension of Wanderer for World of Warcraft Forever. A tab of your spellbook shows everything your trainers still have for you, class spells, weapon skills and demon tomes, and above all what it will cost: now, at your next level and up to the last one, with your gold beside it. It counts only what is worth buying for the way you play, without ever hiding the rest.

Download on CurseForge

A free extension: it needs Wanderer, and the CurseForge app installs both together.

I

How much gold do I need for my next spells?

Three sums make a ledger: what you can learn now, what your next level brings, and everything left up to the last level, with your reputation discount included. Your gold stands beside them with a plain answer: what is missing to learn everything now, or enough up to level 32.

Under it, the spells level by level, each with its own sum, the earlier ranks first. A search field finds a spell in a word.

Beside the sums, a goal of your own: riding at level 40 in one click, an estimate you change to the price you see, or any sum you save for, with what you already have in hand.

II

Can it count only the spells my specialization needs?

Yes. Choose what you play, one specialization or several, the bear and the cat apart for a feral druid: the sums count only what is worth buying for it, as classic-era guides advise. A cat stops paying for every rank of Maul, a feral druid buys only the first rank of Moonfire.

Nothing is locked: the rest stays shown under Outside what you play and can be counted with all its ranks in one click. A spell you never want can be left out, and counted again whenever you like.

III

Are the spell prices right on WoW Forever?

Until your first visit, prices come from the data of What's Training. At your class trainer, Training reads what each spell really costs and keeps it for this character, so the sums follow Forever's own prices.

Rest the mouse on a spell in your spellbook or on your bars: its tooltip names the next rank, the level it comes at and its price. A spell outside what you play says why in one line, for example that one rank is enough for your specialization.

IV

Can I learn everything that counts in one click at the trainer?

Yes. Every trainer opens in a Wanderer window: Learn what I play takes every spell that counts, the cheapest first while your gold lasts, and Learn everything takes the rest too. It says what you will pay and what is left after. The game's window is one click away.

The closest class trainer can be pinned on your map, and Wanderer's town card says when one stands nearby with something for you, never pointing to a capital far away.

V

Can it update the old ranks on my action bars?

New ranks go on your bars by themselves when you learn them, never during a fight, and one click undoes it. A lower rank you put on a bar yourself stays there.

Yes. After learning, a line lists the buttons still using an older rank: Update puts your best rank on them, Undo puts the old ones back, and a right click keeps a rank on purpose. Never during a fight.

Your macros follow: one that names an older rank gets your best one on the same click, nothing else in it changes. On the trainer's rows, each new rank shows what it brings, the first number that changes in its description.

VI

Can I plan my talents up to level 60?

Yes, at any level. The calculator sits beside the game's talent window: click Simulate, then click the talents in the order you want, up to all 51 points. Nothing is spent, and the game's rules apply. The Order tab lists every point level by level, and the next point to spend stands out on the tree.

Hover a talent to see what it changes in your own numbers: damage or healing, cost, cast time, cooldown and range, now, with one more point and at its last rank. Each figure says whether it is read in the game or calculated, and what cannot be worked out stays unknown rather than guessed.

Keep several plans per character, each with a note, one tied to each specialization if you wish. Compare a plan with your talents, another plan or a player of your class you inspected, see it at any level, search the talents that name a spell, and share it as a code.

Questions about Wanderer Training

Does it hide spells from my other specializations?

Never. They stay shown and can be bought or counted at any time. Training only decides what goes into the sums and the Learn what I play button.

Does it work with Naowh Training?

Yes. When Naowh's panel at the trainer is on, Training makes way at class trainers and its own window stays one click away.

Are my choices shared between characters?

No. What you play, the spells left out, the kept ranks and the talent plan belong to each character.

How do I open Wanderer Training?

From its tab in your spellbook, Wanderer's menu, a key of yours, or by typing /wtraining. /wtraining talents opens the planner.

Where does its data come from?

Spells, tomes and trainers come from What's Training (MIT licence), credited in the addon. Wanderer Training is free software under the GPL 3.0.
